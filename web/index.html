What makes it different

You build the path

No fixed lane. Walls and towers reshape the route, and enemies re-plan it live with A*. The game never lets you seal the core off.

Wire for synergy

Adjacent towers link for bonus damage and rate. Some pairs unlock named combos like Superconductor and Dirty Bomb.

10 towers x 3 branches

Each tower has three upgrade paths with a 6-level cap, so you have to specialise.

30 waves, 3 bosses

10 enemy types: flyers that ignore the maze, armored, shielded, splitting, teleporting, healers and hives. A boss arrives every 10 waves.

5 boards, 4 modes

Campaign with stars, Endless, Sandbox and Challenge (Iron Grid, No Walls, Overclock Rush, Glass Core).

Neon, all in code

Every pixel and sound is generated by the game itself. There are no image or audio files.

Controls

Mouse / touchTap a part in the panel, then tap the board. Drag to draw walls. Tap a tower to upgrade, sell or retarget it. Right-click cancels.
Keyboard1-0 towers · W wall · Space start wave · F 1x/2x/3x · Q/E/Z upgrade · S sell · T target · P pause · M mute
